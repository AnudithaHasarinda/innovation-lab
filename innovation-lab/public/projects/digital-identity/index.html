Contacts and leads

People you meet, with a lead score and a follow-up date. Anyone who shares their card back is added automatically.

The lead score is a simple rule: it adds points for each detail you have (email, phone, company, role, where you met, follow-up date). Hot is 70 or more, warm is 40 to 69.
